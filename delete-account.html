Am I Ready? Life Decisions

Account and Data Deletion

You can delete your Am I Ready? Life Decisions profile and the app data stored for it at any time. The current version stores profile and assessment data locally on your device.

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Am I Ready? Life Decisions and sign in.
  2. Open the menu in the upper-right corner.
  3. Select Delete account.
  4. Review the explanation and select Delete permanently.

The deletion is completed directly in the app and does not require contacting support.

What will be deleted

  • Your local app profile, including the name, age, and gender entered in the app.
  • The local link between the app profile and the selected Google or Apple sign-in identity.
  • Saved assessment progress, unfinished assessments, and result history for that profile.
  • The local sign-in session and locally cached Pro status.

Purchases and subscriptions

Deleting the app profile does not cancel or erase purchases made through the App Store or Google Play. Store transactions are controlled by Apple or Google, not by Am I Ready? Life Decisions.
  • Monthly and yearly subscriptions must be cancelled in the relevant store to stop future renewals.
  • A Lifetime purchase remains in the Apple ID or Google Play purchase history and may be restored later.
  • Deleting the profile removes the locally cached Pro status from the app.

Manage subscriptions through Apple or Google Play.

Google and Apple accounts

Deleting your app profile does not delete your Google Account or Apple ID. If you sign in again after deletion, the app creates a new empty local profile and asks for age and gender again.

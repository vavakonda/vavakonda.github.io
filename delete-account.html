Account and Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your Am I Ready? Life Decisions account and associated synchronized data at any time.

How to submit a request

  1. Send an email from the Google email address used to sign in to the App.
  2. Use the subject line: Am I Ready? Life Decisions account deletion.
  3. State that you want your App account and associated synchronized data deleted.
Request account deletion

What will be deleted

  • Your synchronized App profile.
  • Saved assessment progress and result history associated with the account.
  • App authentication sessions controlled by our service.
  • Account-linked Pro entitlement records where deletion is legally permitted.

What may be retained

Limited transaction, fraud-prevention, security, dispute, or legal records may be retained where required. Google Play purchase history is controlled separately by Google and is not deleted by removing the App account.

Local device data

Data stored only on a device can be removed by signing out, clearing the App's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the App.
